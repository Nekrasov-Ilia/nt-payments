A few things
to know.

When is the draw?

The proposed campaign runs for one calendar month, with one draw after entries are checked. Exact opening, closing and draw dates will be published before launch. New registrants would have 14 days to qualify for the welcome bonus, even if they join near month-end.

What is the welcome bonus?

A small guaranteed bonus after a new customer’s first eligible wallet payment, usable towards a future eligible payment. The amount, eligible services, minimum payment and expiry remain to be agreed. There are no additional wallet prize draws in this proposal.

Do I need a bank card?

The proposed journey uses cash funding at supported NT.Payments kiosks. The product team must confirm availability and the exact funding steps before launch.

Is this campaign live?

No. This is an independent programme concept, not an invitation to enter. No registration, payment, raffle entry or bonus is processed on this page. Launch depends on approved rules, promotion permissions and a tested customer journey.
