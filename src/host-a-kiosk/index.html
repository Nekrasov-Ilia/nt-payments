Before you get started

Good questions.
Clear expectations.

Is my business suitable?

Supermarkets, shops, hotels and pharmacies can offer customers a convenient payment stop. Contact us to assess your location and confirm eligibility.

How much space do I need?

Plan for one square metre of space. We’ll confirm dimensions, accessibility and clearance requirements for your location.

How much will I earn?

You receive a share of commission for accepted payments. Your rate, settlement schedule and any costs are agreed in writing. Income varies with payment activity.

Who installs and maintains the kiosk?

We’ll discuss installation, servicing, connectivity, cash handling and maintenance responsibilities before you commit.
